## No Surprises Act Protection
Under federal and state law, emergency services provided at any hospital facility (regardless of in-network or out-of-network status) must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels. Independent out-of-network physicians practicing within a hospital are legally prohibited from balance-billing patients.
