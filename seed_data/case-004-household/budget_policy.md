# Household Financial Budget Policy & Rules (2026)

**Policy ID**: POL-HH-2026  
**Last Updated**: January 1, 2026  

## Rules & Spending Limits
1. **Rule 6.1 (Rate Hike Protection)**: Any recurring vendor subscription or utility rate increase greater than 10% requires a 30-day prior written notice. Unannounced increases must be flagged as unauthorized price hikes.
2. **Rule 6.2 (Recurring Spending Cap)**: Total recurring monthly utility and subscription expenses must not exceed $150.00 / month.
3. **Duplicate Charge Protection**: Any identical subscription debits within the same billing period must be flagged for dispute.
