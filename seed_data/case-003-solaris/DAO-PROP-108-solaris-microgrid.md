# DAO Governance Proposal #108: Community Solar Microgrid Deployment

**Status**: Passed  
**Author**: Green Energy Working Group Lead (@solaris_engineer)  
**Date**: May 15, 2026  
**Proposal ID**: DAO-PROP-108  

## Abstract
Proposal to allocate DAO Treasury capital for the procurement and deployment of a decentralised 150kW solar microgrid system with lithium storage batteries for the regional community center.

## Requested Budget
- **Total Requested Amount**: 75,000 USDC
- **Disbursement Mechanism**: Upfront treasury allocation
- **Recipient Wallet Address**: `0x42B9A88F11029`

## Scope of Work
- Phase 1: High-efficiency solar PV panel installation (75 panels)
- Phase 2: Inverter & battery storage array integration (100 kWh capacity)
- Phase 3: Grid interconnection and remote IoT monitoring sensors
